Our story

CloCourt is menswear for men who let their clothes speak quietly.

We believe a good impression does not need to be loud. It comes from fabric that feels right, a cut that fits well and details that are easy to live with.

Our collection is small on purpose. Every piece is chosen to work with the others and to be worn again and again.

Our philosophy

Quiet luxury, refined and understated. Impression that stays.
